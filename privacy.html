Privacy policy

Nulldelve · Developer: ajavadinezhad · Updated November 24, 2026

Playing on your device

You can play without an account. Checkpoints, equipment, progress, training and settings are saved on your device. We do not include advertising, gameplay analytics, location tracking, or access to your contacts, camera or microphone. Cash in the game is virtual currency, not a real-money purchase.

Optional accounts and cloud saves

Where sign-in is enabled, choosing Continue with Google opens Google sign-in. Google shares your account identifier and email with Supabase, our authentication and database provider, to create or recognize your game account. We do not ask for your Google password. You choose a username, display name and a preset avatar, which we store with your account and show to you in the menu and game. We do not download your Google profile photo. Authentication uses session tokens stored on this device. Supabase processes authentication and service security information such as IP addresses and request logs to operate the service.

After you connect cloud progress, automatic checkpoint uploads store your campaign progress, inventory, cash, skills and checkpoint state under your account. Device display/control settings and sign-in tokens are not included in the cloud save. Your saved progress is private to your account. If another device has changed it, we ask which progress to keep instead of silently replacing it. Offline play remains available; cloud saving needs an internet connection.

Sharing and security

We use Supabase to provide the optional account and cloud-save service. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising. Account traffic uses HTTPS, and database access is restricted to the signed-in account. No system can guarantee absolute security. External links open third-party services such as GitHub or your email app; those services handle your interactions under their own policies. GitHub Pages hosts the public website and receives ordinary web requests when you visit it.

Retention and deletion

Local progress remains until you clear game/site storage or remove it. Signing out removes this device's sign-in session and stops cloud saving, while keeping its local checkpoint. Use Settings → Account → Delete account to permanently delete your account, player identity and cloud progress. This keeps the local checkpoint so you may continue offline. Service logs and provider backups may remain for their operational retention periods; they are not kept as a playable cloud account.

You can also request deletion through our account deletion page or contact us from your account email. If you have an older exported backup, delete that file separately; account deletion cannot remove copies you control.
